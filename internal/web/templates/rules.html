Port rules

Rules tick or untick ports and set their role by a pattern on the port name, description (ifAlias) or the CDP/LLDP neighbour seen on it, so 300 devices do not need 300 rounds of clicking. They apply to ports a discovery finds for the first time; ticks and roles you set by hand stay. Rules are checked from the top and the first match wins. Patterns are regular expressions, case-insensitive: UPLINK|CORE, ^Vlan, ^(Gi|Te)1/1/.

Ports with the role uplink are polled {{if .Fast}}every {{.Fast}} s{{else}}with the rest of the device (the uplink interval is off){{end}}; change. Every port's role is exported as the role label, so Grafana can filter on role="uplink".
